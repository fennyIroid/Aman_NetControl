Bundled host lists: sources and licences
=======================================

adult-hosts.txt and violence-hosts.txt are generated from the "porn" extension of
StevenBlack/hosts (https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts, MIT), fetched 2026-09-24 from
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StevenBlack/hosts/master/alternates/porn-only/hosts

That extension merges the following sources, each under its own licence:

  hostsVN adult (bigdargon)          https://github.com/bigdargon/hostsVN               MIT
  pornhosts (Clefspeare13)            https://github.com/Clefspeare13/pornhosts          MIT
  Sinfonietta pornography hosts       https://github.com/Sinfonietta/hostfiles           MIT
  Sinfonietta snuff hosts             https://github.com/Sinfonietta/hostfiles           MIT
  brijrajparmar27 host-sources Porn   https://github.com/brijrajparmar27/host-sources    CC BY 4.0
  tiuxo hosts - pornography           https://github.com/tiuxo/hosts                     CC BY 4.0

The CC BY 4.0 sources require attribution, which this file provides. The lists were changed:
leading "www." was removed, entries were de-duplicated, the snuff section was split into
violence-hosts.txt, and the entries named in BundledHostLists.NEVER_BLOCK were left out.

The adult-hostname keyword rules in platform/network/AdultHostKeywords.kt are ported from
BlockNSFW (https://github.com/codepurse/BlockNSFW, shared/host-keywords.js), MIT.
